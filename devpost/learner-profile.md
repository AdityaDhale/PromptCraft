# Learner Profile

## Initial Idea
PromptCraft: An AI prompt testing and refinement workbench. The user inputs prompts, tests them across sample inputs, compares outputs side-by-side with visual text diffs, and inspects clarity and conciseness scores.

## Demonstrated Technical and Agent Experience
The learner wants to move directly into building the app with high velocity ("build the app").
Evidence: "build the app".

## Planning-Workflow Experience
Fast-track learner: prefers rapid progression, high efficiency, and minimal overhead while maintaining the mandatory Devpost planning artifacts (scope, PRD, spec).

## Desired Learning Outcome
Build and ship a working proof of concept with clean architecture and all required Devpost hackathon submission assets (working app, demo video readiness, planning documents).

## Interests, Inspirations, and Goals
Targeting the Build With AI: Basics hackathon on Devpost. Goals: Create a functional, self-contained AI developer utility with high visual Polish that demos effectively within 1–3 minutes.

## Vocabulary and Concepts Likely to Need Explanation
Keep explanations concise, focused on key decisions and architecture choices.

## Collaboration and Communication Preferences
- Mode: Fast mode
- Explanation style: Concise, actionable
- Question style: Batched or direct decision points to maximize velocity

## Review Format
markdown

## Notes and Open Questions
